Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and hashed password when you register.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter into the app.
  • Usage data — basic information about how you interact with the service, such as pages visited and actions taken, used solely to improve the app.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive personal data beyond what is listed above.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect exclusively to:

  • Provide and maintain your Spendly account and expense history.
  • Display your spending summaries, category breakdowns, and reports.
  • Improve the reliability and performance of the service.
  • Send essential service communications (e.g., password reset emails).

We will never use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it without your consent except as required by law.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.

You are responsible for keeping your account password secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, please change your password immediately.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. We will process deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly uses Google Fonts to load typefaces (DM Sans and DM Serif Display). This means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when you visit any page. Google's own privacy policy governs any data collected through this request.

Beyond that, we do not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the app. We are committed to addressing your concerns promptly and transparently.
